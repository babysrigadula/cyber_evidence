FAQ

Designed for a controlled academic prototype.

What does this system manage?

It manages cybercrime cases, digital evidence metadata, integrity records, custody events, investigation activities, audit logs, searches and reports.

Does SHA-256 prove that evidence is authentic?

No. A matching hash supports an integrity check; it does not independently establish origin, authenticity or lawful acquisition.

Who can use the platform?

Roles in the project include Administrator, Investigator, Forensic Analyst, Evidence Custodian and Supervisor.
